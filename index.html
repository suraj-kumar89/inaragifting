What happens when they open it

A gift is judged in the first ten seconds. This one is built for those seconds and for the ninety evenings that follow.

  • The weight of the box

    A glass test tube holds 22 incense sticks, paired with a thoughtfully crafted metal holder. Everything is housed within a beautifully finished box, creating a tactile experience from the very first touch.

  • The scent before the sight

    As the lid opens, a gentle wave of fragrance escapes, inviting you in before you even light the incense sticks.

  • The first evening at home

    One stick, 55 minutes, a room that changes. That is when the gift lands.

  • Many evenings later

    Used, finished, replaced. Very few gifts are still in the room this long.
